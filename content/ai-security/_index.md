# AI & Security

This section explores practical ideas at the intersection of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity operations, vulnerability management, ServiceNow SecOps, and OT/ICS security.

The goal is not to present AI as a replacement for security professionals. Instead, this section focuses on how AI can support better triage, prioritization, ownership assignment, communication, and risk translation across technical and operational teams.

## Focus Areas

### AI for Vulnerability Management

Ideas for using AI to help interpret vulnerable items, asset context, business criticality, remediation history, exception patterns, and ownership data.

### AI for ServiceNow SecOps

Concepts for AI-assisted workflows in ServiceNow SecOps, including assignment group recommendation, remediation guidance, risk explanation, and escalation support.

### AI for OT/ICS Risk Translation

Research and ideas focused on translating cybersecurity findings into language that OT operations, engineering, and business stakeholders can act on.

### AI SecOps Idea Lab

A collection of concept-level security ideas that are realistic, professional, and grounded in actual cybersecurity workflows.
