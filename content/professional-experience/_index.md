## Professional Story

My experience is not limited to classroom projects or isolated technical labs.

Before cybersecurity consulting, I worked in building operations where unresolved issues had real-world consequences: tenant impact, operational disruption, safety concerns, equipment problems, contractor coordination, and emergency response.

At Wipro, I saw the same operational pattern in cybersecurity delivery. Vulnerability findings do not matter unless they become accountable work with clear ownership, communication, validation, and closure.

That connection is the center of my professional story.

I am building my career around helping organizations turn technical security work into executable workflows and operational outcomes.
