# Projects

This section contains selected cybersecurity, ServiceNow, academic, and technical projects that demonstrate practical security knowledge, structured problem solving, and hands-on experience.

Many projects are intentionally presented in a **redacted case-study format**. The goal is to show technical depth and decision-making without exposing private data, client information, full solutions, or sensitive implementation details.

Each project may include:

* Problem statement
* Environment and tools used
* My role and responsibilities
* What I built, analyzed, or documented
* Redacted screenshots or diagrams
* Lessons learned
* What I would improve with more time

## Featured Project Areas

### ServiceNow SecOps & Vulnerability Response

Projects and writeups related to vulnerability intake, vulnerable items, ownership assignment, remediation tracking, exception handling, validation, and closure workflows.

### Cybersecurity Coursework

Selected Penn State projects that demonstrate security analysis, risk assessment, network security, incident response, and applied cybersecurity concepts.

### Redacted Project Gallery

A curated collection of sanitized project artifacts, diagrams, screenshots, and summaries designed to show proof of work without publishing full solutions.

### Security Operations & Risk

Projects focused on translating technical findings into operational risk, prioritization, and actionable remediation plans.
