This section contains hands-on labs, technical notes, academic cybersecurity lab collections, ServiceNow SecOps workflow testing, and portfolio-safe research summaries.

The goal is to show practical technical depth without publishing raw academic submissions, full solution files, sensitive screenshots, credentials, private infrastructure details, or anything that should remain confidential.

<div class="sr-callout">

<strong>Publishing approach:</strong> Labs are summarized as redacted case studies, concept notes, workflow maps, and lessons learned. The emphasis is on what was investigated, what tools or concepts were used, and what the work demonstrates professionally.
