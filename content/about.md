# About

I’m Vlad K., a U.S. citizen and Penn State graduate with a B.S. in Cybersecurity Analytics & Operations, graduating Cum Laude with a 3.88 GPA.

My background includes hands-on experience supporting ServiceNow SecOps and Vulnerability Response work in client-facing environments, including requirements workshops, user stories, process flows, UAT support, training documentation, and go-live activities.

I created **sudoRunner** as a cybersecurity portfolio and technical notebook to document projects, lessons learned, and practical security concepts related to vulnerability management, security operations, ServiceNow SecOps, and OT/ICS security.

This site is meant to show proof of work beyond a resume: technical projects, professional reflections, cybersecurity writeups, and examples of how I think through security problems.
