## Connection to Cybersecurity

The strongest connection is not a claim of specialized OT security work. It is an operations-first way of thinking:

* findings need clear ownership
* changes need coordination and documentation
* risk decisions need operational context
* response depends on people, process, and technology
* public evidence should protect sensitive details

Those habits now inform how I approach ServiceNow Vulnerability Response, security operations workflows, and continued OT/ICS learning.
