# OT/ICS Security

This section focuses on operational technology, industrial control systems, cyber-physical risk, and the security challenges that exist where cybersecurity meets real-world operations.

My interest in OT/ICS security comes from academic exposure to ICS and SCADA concepts in simulated smart grid environments, where I applied cybersecurity frameworks and risk-based thinking to systems that require availability, safety, and operational continuity.

## Focus Areas

### Cyber-Physical Risk

OT/ICS security is not only about vulnerabilities and technical findings. It is also about understanding how cyber risk can affect physical processes, operations, safety, uptime, and business continuity.

### Risk Frameworks

This section includes notes and projects related to frameworks such as NIST CSF and IEC 62443, especially where they apply to industrial environments and operational security programs.

### Smart Grid and Critical Infrastructure Concepts

Writeups related to smart grid security, industrial network segmentation, control system risk, asset context, and the unique challenges of securing operational environments.

### Translating Cybersecurity for Operations Teams

A major challenge in OT/ICS security is communicating technical findings in a way that engineers, operators, and business stakeholders can act on. This section explores how security teams can translate vulnerability and risk information into operationally meaningful language.
