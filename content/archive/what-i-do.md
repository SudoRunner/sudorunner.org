# I help translate vulnerability findings into accountable security workflows.

My main professional focus is **ServiceNow SecOps and Vulnerability Response**.

The problem I care about is simple: many organizations are good at finding vulnerabilities, but struggle to turn those findings into clear ownership, remediation progress, validation, and closure.

A vulnerability finding does not reduce risk by itself.

Risk is reduced when someone owns the work, understands the priority, takes action, validates the fix, and documents the outcome.

## Why This Matters

Security teams do not fail only because they lack data.

They often fail because the workflow after detection is unclear.

Common problems include:

- findings with no clear owner
- remediation work that is hard to track
- vulnerability records closed too early
- exceptions handled inconsistently
- business impact not clearly understood
- security and infrastructure teams working from different views of the same issue

That is why I am interested in ServiceNow SecOps.

The platform matters because it can help organizations create structure around ownership, workflow, and accountability.

## What I Learned From Wipro

My strongest professional experience came from supporting a **ServiceNow Vulnerability Response client engagement at Wipro**.

That project taught me that cybersecurity delivery is not only technical.

It also depends on:

- stakeholder alignment
- clear requirements
- well-written user stories
- RAID tracking
- daily coordination
- UAT readiness
- training support
- go-live discipline

I entered that project early in my career and was trusted with project coordination responsibilities. That experience changed how I think about consulting.

The biggest lesson was that successful security implementations are delivery achievements, not just technical achievements.
