<span class="sr-eyebrow">Portfolio Design System</span>

This page explains the badge system used across `sudoRunner`.

Badges are used to make project status, content type, professional focus, and portfolio relevance easier to scan.

<div class="sr-callout">

<strong>Purpose:</strong> The badge system helps visitors quickly understand whether a page is a hands-on lab, completed case study, concept note, specialty focus, or privacy-conscious portfolio item.

</div>

---

## Badge Types

<div class="sr-card-grid">

<div class="sr-card">

### Completed / Portfolio-Safe

<span class="sr-status sr-status-complete">Completed</span>

Used for completed credentials, finished portfolio pages, stable project summaries, and finalized case-study content.

</div>

<div class="sr-card">

### In Progress / Specialty Focus

<span class="sr-status sr-status-progress">In Progress</span>

Used for active learning areas, specialty interests, planned improvements, or professional focus areas still being expanded.

</div>

<div class="sr-card">

### Concept / Idea Lab

<span class="sr-status sr-status-concept">Concept</span>

Used for AI security ideas, future lab concepts, design ideas, and non-production research notes.

</div>

<div class="sr-card">

### Hands-On Lab

<span class="sr-status sr-status-lab">Hands-On Lab</span>

Used for lab-based work, practical exercises, technical investigations, and hands-on security or software projects.

</div>

<div class="sr-card">

### Academic Honor

<span class="sr-cum-laude">Cum Laude</span>

Used for academic distinction and education-related highlights.

</div>

</div>

---

## How Badges Are Used

<div class="sr-card-grid">

<div class="sr-card">

### Projects

Badges identify whether a project is ServiceNow-focused, cybersecurity-focused, academic, software engineering-related, OT/ICS-related, or web design-related.

<span class="sr-status sr-status-complete">Project Context</span>

</div>

<div class="sr-card">

### Research & Labs

Badges separate hands-on lab work from conceptual notes, future lab ideas, and active focus areas.

<span class="sr-status sr-status-lab">Lab Context</span>

</div>

<div class="sr-card">

### Credentials

Badges show completed credentials, scheduled exams, academic achievements, and credential progress.

<span class="sr-status sr-status-progress">Credential Status</span>

</div>

<div class="sr-card">

### Capabilities

Badges help connect professional capabilities to evidence across projects, labs, and portfolio pages.

<span class="sr-status sr-status-concept">Skill Mapping</span>

</div>

</div>
